    About this tool

    Is my .bib uploaded anywhere?
    No. All parsing and cleaning happen in your browser. DOI lookups go straight from your browser to the public Crossref API, so your library is never sent to our servers.
    What does “Protect Title Capitals” do?
    BibTeX lowercases title words unless they're wrapped in braces. This wraps acronyms and all-caps words (e.g. DNA, NASA) in {} so they keep their capitalization.
    How does it find a missing DOI?
    It searches Crossref by your entry's title and first author, then checks the top matches against your title, author, and year. It only writes a DOI when a match clears a confidence bar, so it will leave a DOI blank rather than guess wrong. Some works have no DOI at all (many books, theses, and older papers), and those stay blank.
    Where does DOI autocomplete get data?
    The free Crossref REST API (and DataCite for Zenodo-style DOIs). No API key, no cost, rate-limited politely.
